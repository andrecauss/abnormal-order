02
Inventory Planning
"How do we translate demand into inventory and replenishment requirements?"
S&OP · Supply Review
← Input: Unconstrained Demand (Unconstrained Forecast) from Demand Planning
Key activities
  • Define inventory strategy & policies
  • Cover non-forecastable items / long tail
  • Set safety stock & targets
  • Design replenishment approach
  • Generate unconstrained purchase plan
Important concept
Demand Planning concentrates forecast effort on forecastable items; non‑forecastable / long‑tail items are covered mainly through inventory policy.

05
Constrained Plan & Business Decisions
"Can we execute the plan as-is? If not, what will it cost to achieve the required service level?"
S&OP · Reconciliation & Executive
← Supplier & Factory Constraints
← Warehouse / Logistics Constraints
Key activities
  • Consolidate all constraints & identify gaps between plan and capacity
  • Quantify service & financial impact
  • Build constrained plan scenarios and quantify additional costs
  • Present scenarios to Board / Leadership & select preferred scenario
Scenarios for decision
⚠ LACK OF CAPACITY
  • Customer service impact
  • Potential shortage
  • Delayed supply
  • Risk to revenue
MAKE / BUY / EXPEDITE
  • Additional cost
  • Spot orders / expediting
  • Additional capacity
  • Extra operational effort
CUSTOMER SERVICE × COST × CAPACITY
